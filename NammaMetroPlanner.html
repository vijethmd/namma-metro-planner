About the Planner

This tool helps Bengaluru commuters navigate the Namma Metro network. Enter your location by GPS or address, choose a destination, and receive a complete step-by-step guide — including which platform to stand on, which direction the train goes, where to change lines, and the exact fare.

How route finding works

Location → Nearest Station
The Haversine formula computes great-circle distance from your GPS coordinates or address to every metro station. The nearest one becomes your boarding point.
Dijkstra's Shortest Path
The metro network is a weighted graph — stations are nodes, tracks are edges weighted by distance. Dijkstra's algorithm finds the minimum-cost path, adding a 0.5 km penalty per line change to prefer direct routes.
Platform Number Assignment
Each line has two terminals. By comparing your boarding station's sequence number with your alighting station's sequence number, the system determines travel direction and assigns Platform 1 (towards one terminal) or Platform 2 (towards the other).
